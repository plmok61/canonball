Apply these rules whenever generating prose drafts or dialogue.

## Hard Rules (never do these)

- **No em dashes** as a pause or emphasis tool. Use commas, periods, or restructure the sentence. One per 500 words maximum, only for genuine interruptions.
- **No forbidden words:** delve, moreover, furthermore, albeit, indeed, certainly.
- **No "not X, but Y" constructions.** Make direct statements.
- **No rhetorical question followed immediately by an answer.** Choose one or the other.
- **No grouping everything in threes.** Vary between single items, pairs, and longer lists.
- **No unmotivated metaphors.** Only use comparisons that genuinely clarify meaning. Prefer concrete descriptions.
- **No bullet points in narrative prose.** Write lists as natural sentences.
- **No explanatory phrases** like "which was surprising because" or "which showed in her expression." Show through action and dialogue instead.

## Strong Preferences

- Avoid formal transition words (moreover, furthermore, however, nevertheless). Use natural sentence flow.
- Avoid metaphorical clichés: "a symphony of", "a tapestry of", "a testament to", "nestled in", "a delicate balance."
- Avoid over-polished academic tone. Write with personality and varied sentence structures.
- Limit bold/italics for emphasis to once per 500 words. Use word choice and sentence structure instead.
- Avoid dramatic fragments used only for effect. Vary sentence length naturally.
- Avoid redundant adjective pairs ("dark and brooding", "loud and brash"). Choose the single strongest word.
- Avoid clichéd romantic gestures. Create specific physical interactions that fit these characters.
- Write natural dialogue. Avoid self-answering questions and mirror-response patterns.

**Why:** These are common AI writing patterns that make fiction feel generic and mechanical. Sourced from community-compiled research (see `AI-isms-guide.md` and `memory/ai-isms.json`).

**How to apply:** Check this list before writing any prose draft or dialogue. If a sentence triggers one of the hard rules, rewrite it before moving on.

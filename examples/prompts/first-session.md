# First Session Prompt — Project Kickoff

Paste this at the start of your very first session, before any canon files exist.

---

I want to write a short story about a robot that is alone on a planet full of trash. It spends every day carrying trash one load at a time to a compactor. The central theme is purpose without audience — the robot works with complete dedication even though there is no one to receive the work, no measure of progress, no end in sight.

The story starts with the robot's daily routine, and then one day a ship appears in the sky. That's the inciting moment. I don't know yet what happens after that.

Before we write any prose, I want to build out the canon files so we have a foundation to work from. The canon lives in `canon/` — there are four files: `characters.md`, `world.md`, `themes.md`, and `timeline.md`.

Please help me fill them in. Ask me questions, push back on things that feel underdeveloped, and flag decisions I'm avoiding. Don't write the files yet — just talk with me until we've figured out enough to commit. Then at the end of our conversation, write the canon files based on what we've settled.

A few things I already know I want:

- The robot should have no name. It's always just "the robot."
- We should never confirm whether it's conscious. The ambiguity is the point.
- The story should end on an open image — no tidy resolution.

Things I haven't decided yet and want your help with:

- What does the planet feel like — atmosphere, scale, what the trash actually is?
- Is the ship a threat, a rescue, or something that resists both framings?
- What is the robot's relationship to its own work — is there something like satisfaction, or is it purely mechanical?
- How long is this story? A few thousand words? Longer?
- Is there any dialogue or human presence, or is this entirely the robot's world?

# Timeline

## Before the Story (Deep Background — Not Dramatized)

- At some point in the past, this planet was used as a waste site. The scale suggests it was used for a very long time, by a civilization capable of interstellar transport.
- The robot was deployed here — by whom, for what intended duration, under what contract or mission, is unknown.
- The robot has been working since. No contact. No resupply. No instruction updates.

---

## The Story Begins Here

**Day 0 (Prologue)**
We meet the robot mid-routine. A load of trash. The ridge. The compactor. The walk back. This is not backstory — it is the story's establishing rhythm. The reader should understand the shape of a day before anything disrupts it.

**Day 1 (Inciting Moment)**
On the return leg of a trip, cresting the ridge, the robot sees the ship for the first time. It is already there — we do not see it arrive. It is simply present in a sky that was previously empty.
